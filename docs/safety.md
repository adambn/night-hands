# Safety

Short list. Read it once, follow it always.

**Power**
- Wall adapter only. **No LiPo batteries** on this project — not worth the risk
  with a 7-year-old, and we never need to be untethered.
- Unplug the 6 V before changing any wiring. Every time.
- Grounds common, polarity checked twice before first power-on.

**Motors**
- **Set the torque limit before the hand ever touches a person.** Start low
  (~25%) and only raise it if the fingers can't close.
- Fingers pinch. Keep the robot's fingers away from real fingers while a gesture
  is running. "Hands behind your back when it's moving" is a good habit.
- Long hair, sleeves and cables stay away from the shoulder joint.

**Tools**
- Goggles for cutting brass rod, filing, and drilling — both of you.
- The soldering iron and the heat-set iron are Adam's until Jonathan has done a
  practice kit. Then: iron at 300 °C, fan on, goggles, Adam's hand on the iron
  for the first five joints.
- Hot glue burns. It's the sneakiest injury on the bench.

**Sanity**
- Stop while it's still fun. A 90-minute session that ends well beats a
  3-hour session that ends in a tantrum — for either of you.
